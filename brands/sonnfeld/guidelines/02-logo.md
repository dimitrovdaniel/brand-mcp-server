# Sonnfeld: logo

## Versions
- **Primary**: coral sun over a navy horizon line with a navy wordmark. For white, sand and sky surfaces.
- **Inverse**: coral sun with white line and white wordmark. For navy surfaces and evening-sky photography.
- **Mark**: the sun and line alone. Icons and avatars.

## Clear space
At least **half the logo height** on every side.

## Minimum size
- Digital: 100 px wide for the full logo (80 px in ads), 24 px for the mark.
- Print: 28 mm for the full logo, 6 mm for the mark.

## Placement
Top left or top centre. Stories use top centre.

## Don'ts
- Never change the sun colour; it is always Coral 500.
- No glows, gradients or lens flares around the sun.
- Never on busy beach photography; use calm sky areas or solid surfaces.
