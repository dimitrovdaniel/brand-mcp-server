# Sonnfeld: advertising

## Booked formats
Medium rectangle, leaderboard, half page, billboard, Instagram feed and story, DOOH landscape. No mobile banner: the protection category does not fit.

## Structure of a display ad
1. Logo top left or top centre (at least 80 px wide).
2. Headline in Source Serif 4.
3. SPF with protection category as live text, e.g. „Sehr hoher Schutz · LSF 50+“.
4. CTA in `color.action.primary` (Coral 700) with white text.

## Backgrounds
White, sand or navy. White and sand ads get a 1 px `color.border.default` border.

## Seasonality
Campaigns run March to September. DOOH near beaches and pools uses the evening look.

## Animation
At most 15 seconds and 3 loops. No sun flares or flashing light effects.
