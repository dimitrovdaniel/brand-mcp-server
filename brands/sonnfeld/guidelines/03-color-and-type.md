# Sonnfeld: colour and typography

## Colour roles
| Role | Token | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Page background | `color.surface.default` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Sections | `color.surface.subtle` | Sand 50 `#FBF7F0` |
| Brand blocks, evening look | `color.surface.brand` | Navy 900 `#0F2240` |
| Info boxes | `color.surface.accent` | Sky 100 `#DCEEF9` |
| Body text | `color.text.default` | Navy 900 `#0F2240` |
| Primary button | `color.action.primary` | Coral 700 `#A8402A` |
| Sun, icons | `color.accent.default` | Coral 500 `#E2603F` |

## Day and evening
Two looks: **day** (white and sand with navy text and coral accents) and **evening** (navy surfaces with white or sky text and coral accents). Do not mix both in one layout.

## Contrast
Coral 500 on white is for icons and display type only (3.5:1). Body links and buttons use Coral 700. Sky on white is not allowed.

## Typefaces
- **Source Serif 4** SemiBold for headlines. Sentence case.
- **Source Sans 3** for body, UI and all product information.
- Fallbacks: Georgia and Arial.

## Type scale
Display 56 px, headline 40 px, title 28 px, body 16 px, caption 14 px. Line length at most 68 characters. Product information (SPF, UVA seal, water resistance) is never smaller than 14 px.
