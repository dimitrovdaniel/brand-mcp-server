# Sonnfeld: imagery and layout

## Photography
People in light shade or soft morning/evening light, wearing hats and shirts as well as sunscreen. Children always with protective clothing. No sunburn, no tanning poses, no midday glare.

## Product shots
On sand or sky backgrounds, front label readable, SPF clearly visible.

## Layout
- 12-column grid, calm and spacious.
- Info boxes on `color.surface.accent` for usage advice (“So wenden Sie Sonnenschutz richtig an”).
- Logo top left or top centre.
- `radius.md` (8 px) for cards and buttons.

## Accessibility
- Body text at least 16 px; tap targets at least 44 × 44 px.
- The SPF and protection category must be readable as text, not only inside a product image.
