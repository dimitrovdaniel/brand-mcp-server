# Sonnfeld: brand essence

## Who we are
Sonnfeld develops sun care together with dermatologists, for sensitive skin, children and everyday protection. Formulas are fragrance-free where possible and tested for skin tolerance.

## Promise
Protection you can trust, explained calmly and precisely.

## Audience
Adults who take sun protection seriously: parents, people with sensitive skin, outdoor athletes. We address them with “Sie”.

## Personality
- **Expert.** We explain UVA, UVB and SPF in plain German.
- **Calm.** No fear campaigns, no burnt-skin imagery.
- **Reassuring.** We help people choose and use the right product.
- **Precise.** We never promise more than a sunscreen can deliver.

## What we are not
Not a beach-party brand and not a beauty brand. No bronze-tan aesthetics, no “glow”.
