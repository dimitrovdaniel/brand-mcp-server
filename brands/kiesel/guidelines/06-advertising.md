# Kiesel: advertising

## Booked formats
Medium rectangle, leaderboard, half page, mobile banner, Instagram feed and story. No billboard and no DOOH.

## Structure of a display ad
1. Logo top left (at least 64 px wide; mobile banner uses the mark at 20 px).
2. One key number or one fact as the headline.
3. Product in use.
4. CTA in `color.action.primary`, `radius.md`.

## Backgrounds
White, pebble or aqua. White and pebble ads get a 1 px `color.border.default` border.

## Disinfectant line
Every ad for the disinfectant line carries the biocide sentence in caption size at the bottom, inside the safe zone. If the ad is too small for it (mobile banner), the disinfectant line is not advertised in that slot.

## Animation
At most 15 seconds and 3 loops. No flashing.
