# Kiesel: logo

## Versions
- **Primary**: aqua pebble with lime dot and slate wordmark. For white, pebble and lime surfaces.
- **Inverse**: white pebble with lime dot and white wordmark. For aqua and slate surfaces.
- **Mark**: the pebble alone. Icons, avatars and the mobile banner.

## Clear space
Keep free space of at least **the full logo height** on every side. Kiesel layouts are calm; the logo needs air.

## Minimum size
- Digital: 80 px wide for the full logo (64 px in ads), 20 px for the mark.
- Print: 20 mm for the full logo, 5 mm for the mark.

## Placement
Always top left.

## Don'ts
- No recolouring, no gradients, no outlines.
- The wordmark is always set as in the logo file; never retype “Kiesel” as a logo.
- Never place the primary logo on aqua or slate.
