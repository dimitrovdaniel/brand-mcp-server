# Kiesel: imagery and layout

## Photography
Real homes, natural light, a little lived-in. Product in use: hand, cloth, surface. Neutral colour grading, no blue “hygiene” tint, no sparkle effects.

## Diagrams
Simple bar comparisons and pictograms in aqua and slate for numbers (plastic saved, refills per pouch). Every diagram has a source line in caption size.

## Layout
- 12-column grid, generous white space.
- Sharp geometry: `radius.md` (4 px) at most, except pill-shaped tags.
- One key number per layout.
- Logo top left, always.

## Accessibility
- Body text at least 16 px; tap targets at least 44 × 44 px.
- Charts need a text alternative with the same numbers.
