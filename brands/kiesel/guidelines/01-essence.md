# Kiesel: brand essence

## Who we are
Kiesel makes household cleaners in refill pouches and glass bottles, plus a small disinfectant line. We are engineers at heart: we measure, we test against standards, and we publish the numbers.

## Promise
Clean homes with less packaging, proven by numbers instead of promises.

## Audience
Households who want to reduce plastic without compromising on cleaning results. Practical, sceptical of green-washing. We address them with “du”.

## Personality
- **Clear.** One fact per sentence.
- **Factual.** Numbers, standards and ingredients instead of adjectives.
- **Calm.** No exclamation marks, no urgency, no “Power”.
- **Quietly confident.** We let the test results speak.

## What we are not
Not an eco-guilt brand and not a hygiene-fear brand. No germs with faces, no shiny-kitchen clichés, no green leaves as decoration.
