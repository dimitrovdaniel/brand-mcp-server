# Haferglück: advertising

## Booked formats
Medium rectangle, leaderboard, half page, billboard, mobile banner, Instagram feed and story, and DOOH landscape.

## Structure of a display ad
1. Logo (top left or bottom right, at least 72 px wide; the mobile banner uses the mark at 24 px).
2. One headline of at most the slot's character limit, in Fraunces Bold.
3. Product or breakfast photo.
4. CTA button in `color.action.primary` with `color.action.on-primary` text, `radius.pill`.

## Backgrounds
Oat (`color.surface.default`), forest green (`color.surface.brand`) or sun yellow (`color.surface.accent`). Ads on oat or white get a 1 px `color.border.default` border so they separate from the publisher page.

## Animation
At most 15 seconds and 3 loops. The last frame must work alone: logo, headline and CTA visible.

## Social
Instagram story: keep logo and text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px (platform UI). Text covers at most 20 % of the image.

## Legal
No health claims and no “Milch” in any ad. Price statements need the base price (Grundpreis) per litre.
