# Haferglück: brand essence

## Who we are
Haferglück makes oat drinks and granola from oats grown in Bavaria. The company is small, the oats travel 120 km from field to bottle, and the recipes have few ingredients. That is the whole story, and we tell it with a smile.

## Promise
Breakfast that tastes like a good morning. Simple ingredients, honest numbers, and a bit of fun at the table.

## Audience
Young families, students and people new to plant drinks. They are curious and price-aware, and they distrust big claims. We address them with “du”.

## Personality
- **Warm.** We sound like a friend at the breakfast table, not a nutrition label.
- **Cheeky, not silly.** One joke per piece is plenty. Never at someone's expense.
- **Down-to-earth.** We talk about oats, Bavaria and breakfast, not superfoods.
- **Honest about ingredients.** We say what is in it and stop there. No health promises.

## What we are not
Not a wellness brand, not an eco lecture, not luxury. Avoid sterile white-lab imagery, “clean eating” language and anything that shames other diets.
