# Haferglück: imagery and layout

## Photography
Real breakfast tables in daylight, slightly from above. Hands, crumbs and spills are welcome. Warm colour grading; no cold, blue-tinted shots. People are shown eating, laughing or pouring, never posing with the pack.

## Product shots
Pack on oat or sun-yellow backgrounds with a soft shadow. Front label always readable. No floating packs, no splash effects.

## Illustration
Simple line illustrations of grains, suns and spoons in forest green. Used sparingly as decoration, never for information.

## Layout
- 12-column grid, 24 px gutters on desktop, 16 px on mobile.
- Generous spacing: sections are separated by at least `space.7` (48 px).
- Rounded corners: cards `radius.md` (12 px), buttons `radius.pill`.
- One key message per screen or ad.

## Accessibility
- Body text at least 16 px; tap targets at least 44 × 44 px.
- Every image that carries information needs alt text in German.
- Do not put text over photos without a solid surface behind it.
