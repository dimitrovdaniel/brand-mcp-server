# Haferglück: voice and copy

## Address
Always “du”. Never “Sie”, never “ihr” for one person.

## Tone
Short sentences. Concrete words. One wink per piece. We prefer “Hafer aus Bayern, 120 km bis zur Flasche” to “nachhaltig”.

## Words we use
Haferdrink, Granola, Frühstück, Hafer aus Bayern, wenige Zutaten, schmeckt.

## Words we never use
- “Hafermilch” or “Milch”: plant drinks must not be called milk under EU law. Say “Haferdrink”.
- “gesund”: a general health claim that needs an authorised specific claim. Describe ingredients instead.
- “klimaneutral”: we do not make this claim.
- “100 % natürlich”: hard to prove. Say “ohne Zuckerzusatz” or “4 Zutaten” when true.

## Brand name
Write “Haferglück” as one word with ü. Not “Hafer Glück”, not “HAFERGLÜCK” in running text, and not “Haferglueck” except in URLs.

## Punctuation
At most one exclamation mark per piece. No emoji in headlines. German quotation marks „…“ in body copy.

## Calls to action
“Jetzt probieren”, “Zum Rezept”, “Im Shop ansehen”, “Mehr erfahren”. 20 characters at most.

## Examples
- Good: „Hafer aus Bayern. Vier Zutaten. Ein guter Morgen.“
- Good: „Dein Müsli hat einen neuen besten Freund.“
- Bad: „Die gesunde Hafermilch für Sie!!“ (milk, health claim, Sie, two exclamation marks)
