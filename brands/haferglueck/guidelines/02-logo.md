# Haferglück: logo

## Versions
- **Primary**: forest-green grain and wordmark with a sun-yellow sun. For oat, white and sun-yellow surfaces.
- **Inverse**: white grain and wordmark with a sun-yellow sun. For forest-green surfaces and dark, calm photo areas.
- **Mark**: the grain and sun without the wordmark. For app icons, favicons, social avatars and the mobile banner. Never place the mark next to the full logo.

## Clear space
Keep free space of at least **half the logo height** on every side. Nothing else (text, product, image edges) may enter this space.

## Minimum size
- Digital: 96 px wide for the full logo, 24 px for the mark. In ads the full logo may go down to 72 px.
- Print: 25 mm wide for the full logo, 6 mm for the mark.

## Placement
Top left or bottom right. On the leaderboard the logo sits on the left.

## Don'ts
- Do not recolour the logo, add outlines or shadows, or rotate it.
- Do not put the primary logo on forest green or the inverse logo on oat.
- Do not set “Haferglück” in another typeface as a replacement for the logo.
- Do not place the logo over busy photo areas; use a calm area or a solid surface.
